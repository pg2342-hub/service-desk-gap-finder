Service desk gap finder

A support desk's knowledge base and its ticket notes often disagree, and the real fix tends to sit in the notes, often the outside provider's. This tool reads a year of resolved tickets next to the articles, lists every fix the articles are missing, and then applies one gate to every topic: it may answer on its own only after 48 of its last 50 drafted answers were approved with no edits.

Where the missing fixes live

Each row is one step the articles were missing. The dots show how often that step appears in the provider's notes and in the internal team's notes for the same topic. A wide gap means the fix is only written down by the provider, so it would be lost if the provider changed.

The rules

Point-of-sale outages, payments and security incidents are never answered automatically, whatever their drafts score. They are drafted for an analyst or routed straight to a person.

Data

All tickets, topics, steps and volumes are made up for a fictional retailer's employee IT desk and describe no real company. Each resolution note carries tagged steps, so the tool counts steps rather than reading free text. A simulated analyst approves a draft only when it has exactly the steps that ticket needed.

Run it yourself

Python 3.9 or later, standard library only. From the repo folder:

python3 run.py
python3 -m unittest
python3 scripts/build_demo.py
